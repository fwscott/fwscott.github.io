What we measure

We agree on a baseline and a target before building anything.

Manual workload
Time spent rekeying data, processing email, and maintaining spreadsheets.
Turnaround time
How long inquiries, proposals, and booking handoffs take.
Errors and rework
Corrections, missed follow-ups, and exceptions that need redoing.
Advisor capacity
Admin time per booking and the time returned to client work.
Deadline risk
Overdue tasks, and whether every critical handoff has an owner.
System handoffs
Duplicate entry and failed transfers between CRM, back office, and GDS.

Results depend on your systems, workflow, and adoption. These are measures we track together, not guaranteed gains.

Common questions

Do you only work with group travel agencies?

No. I work with travel agencies and tour operators on workflow automation, AI implementation, and custom software. Group air, tours, and GDS operations are specialist experience, but email processing, reporting, and system handoffs matter across travel businesses.

What can AI automate in a travel agency?

Useful starting points include classifying incoming emails, extracting details from supplier documents, and drafting internal summaries for review. Rules-based automation is often better for predictable tasks. Booking changes, payments, and client commitments need appropriate controls and human approval.

Can you work with our existing CRM, back-office tools, or GDS?

The assessment starts with your current tools, including Amadeus or Sabre where relevant. Integration options depend on API access, permissions, vendor terms, and data quality. We confirm those constraints before proposing a build; replacing everything is not the default.

How much does travel technology consulting cost?

Fees depend on the workflow, systems involved, and implementation scope. After an initial conversation, we agree on deliverables and fees before starting. A focused assessment can establish scope before you commit to a larger implementation.

How do you approach traveler data and AI risk?

We identify sensitive data, access requirements, and vendor handling policies before choosing tools. The design should minimize data sharing and define review, exception handling, and access controls. Do not include traveler records, passport details, or payment data in your initial inquiry.
